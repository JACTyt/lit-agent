# Moral and Lesson Extraction Guide

## Goal
Extract the central lesson, moral, or takeaway from a story in one clear sentence.

## Procedure
- Identify the main conflict or transformation.
- Look for repeated outcomes, consequences, or explicit lesson statements.
- Summarize the takeaway in simple language.
- Keep the moral general enough to apply beyond the story.

## Good Moral Style
- "Hard work and preparation lead to better outcomes than procrastination."
- "Kindness and humility build stronger relationships than pride."
- "Small acts of help can make a large difference over time."

## Avoid
- Overly vague statements.
- Repeating the plot instead of the lesson.
- Adding a moral that is not supported by the text.

## Consistency Rule
If the same book is classified again and the evidence has not changed, the moral should remain the same or very close in wording.
