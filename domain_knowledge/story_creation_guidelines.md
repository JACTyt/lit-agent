# Story Creation Guidelines

When users ask to create a book or story, prioritize narrative quality over short confirmations.

## Required Story Qualities
- Include a clear protagonist with a goal.
- Include at least two supporting characters with distinct roles.
- Establish a vivid setting with concrete sensory details.
- Present a concrete conflict with stakes.
- Show rising action, a turning point, and meaningful resolution.
- Integrate the moral through character actions, not only a closing sentence.

## Style Expectations
- Use scenes and occasional dialogue.
- Prefer specific verbs and images over generic phrases.
- Keep the ending satisfying and emotionally coherent.
- For children's stories, keep language clear while still imaginative.

## Output Priorities
- For creation requests: persist the story first, then provide path and confirmation.
- Avoid replacing the narrative with only metadata-style summaries.
