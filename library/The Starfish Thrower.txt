Title: The Starfish Thrower
Genre: short story
Theme: compassion and small acts of kindness
Audience: general
Reading level: easy
Moral: Small acts of help matter, even when the larger problem seems overwhelming.

Story:
An old man was walking on the beach at dawn. After a big storm, thousands of starfish had been washed up on the sand. In the distance, he saw a young boy picking them up one by one and throwing them back into the ocean.
The man walked up to the boy and said, "Son, there are miles of beach and thousands of starfish. You can’t possibly make a difference."
The boy looked at the starfish in his hand, tossed it into the waves, and replied, "It made a difference to that one."
