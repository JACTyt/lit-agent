# Narrative Patterns

## Common Story Patterns
- Problem and solution: a character faces a challenge and resolves it.
- Lesson through consequence: a poor choice creates a result that teaches a lesson.
- Small act of kindness: a minor action produces an outsized positive effect.
- Growth through change: a character becomes wiser, calmer, or more humble.
- Comparison or contrast: two approaches are shown to highlight the better one.

## What to Look For
- Who changes during the story?
- What conflict drives the plot?
- What outcome does the story reward or punish?
- Does the text signal a lesson explicitly or implicitly?

## How to Use Patterns
- Use the pattern to support classification.
- Use the pattern to infer the moral.
- Use the pattern to keep summaries concise and consistent.

## Pattern-to-Genre Hints
- Lesson through consequence often maps to fable or moral tale.
- Small act of kindness often maps to short story or moral tale.
- Growth through change often maps to realism or moral tale.
- Problem and solution with symbolic characters often maps to fable.
